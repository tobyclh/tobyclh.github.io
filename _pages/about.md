I am a principal researcher at **TOEI Zukun Research**. We create stylized, production-ready character facial animation, and are always interested in collaborating with eager interns and research labs. We also provide high-quality character facial capture and animation services for industry and academia, as well as light-stage-based 3D scanning. Please get in touch if you are interested.

I received my PhD and MSc in Creative Informatics under [Takeo Igarashi](https://www-ui.is.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/~takeo/index-j.html), and a BEng in Electrical Engineering under [Koji Yatani](https://iis-lab.org/), all from the University of Tokyo.

My research focuses on character facial animation, capture, virtual try-on, and human–computer interaction.
